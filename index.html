How it works

No auditions. No door fee for performers. No filters on what "counts" as a song.

  1. 01

    Show up early

    Sign-ups open thirty minutes before the first set, first come first served. Solo, duo, or full band — whatever you've got.

  2. 02

    Play fifteen minutes

    Two or three songs, unplugged. Covers, originals, half-finished ideas — all welcome on this stage.

  3. 03

    Stay for the room

    The best part isn't your set, it's everyone else's. Most people stay long after their own turn is over.
